What does our Vacation Planner do?>

This Android application aims to ease the vacation planning process by having a local data storage solution to access your vacation details with or without an internet connection! You can copy vacation details to send to others, or set notifications to remind you of what you have to do that day of the vacation. If you have been looking for a way to make vacation planning easier and reliable, you came to the right place.
